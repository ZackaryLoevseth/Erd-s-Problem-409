# Limitations

- This is a finite pointwise certificate.
- Priority and world-record status are not claimed.
- The release does not prove unboundedness, a global maximum, a density result,
  basin infinitude, or inverse-tree completeness for the F=104 head.
- The original SymPy and trial-division evaluators share the trajectory data;
  the third audit changes factorization and primality methods but still uses
  ordinary integer arithmetic and the exported certificate.
- Internal AI-assisted validation is not external specialist review.
- OEIS and Erdős Problems links are citations, not verification authority.
